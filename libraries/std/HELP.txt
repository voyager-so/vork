Scene geometry uses its table key or inline position for identity. Shapes are centred;
dimensions are metres and size is the full XYZ extent. Negative or non-finite values
are errors. Rounded-box radii fit half the smallest extent. Bevel segments round up
to an even count, preserving exact planar faces. Extrusion rims fit depth/2 and
profile clearance. Box faces span 0..1 UV. Round shapes use Y as axis; cylinder
has both caps, cone has a bottom cap, capsule height includes caps, and torus
radius measures to the tube centre. Round UVs run from +X toward -Z around Y
and top to bottom. Grid lies in XZ with +Y normals; size is its full XZ extent.
Sweep scale must be positive; closed paths require matching endpoint scales and whole turns of twist.
SVG extrusion uses XY metres without flipping Y.
